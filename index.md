# Dream Theater Studio

### Original Vertical Drama Production & International Distribution

Dream Theater Studio is an original digital entertainment brand operated by **Putian Huanzhen Cultural Media Co., Ltd.**, a cultural media company established in China on **September 9, 2026**.

We focus on the development, production, localization, and international distribution of original vertical short-form drama content for global audiences.

---

## About Us

Our production team combines original storytelling with AI-assisted audiovisual production technologies to create cinematic vertical dramas designed for international digital platforms.

Our production workflow includes:

- Original story development and screenwriting
- Character and environment design
- AI-assisted audiovisual production
- Video editing and post-production
- English localization
- International content distribution and operations

Our content is primarily developed for English-speaking and international audiences.
